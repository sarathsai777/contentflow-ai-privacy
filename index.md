# ContentFlow AI — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction

ContentFlow AI is an independent academic software project that helps users transform text, documents, audio, and video into social media content.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when using ContentFlow AI.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, ContentFlow AI may process:

- Account information, such as name and email address.
- Organization and team information.
- Content uploaded by users, including documents, text, audio, and video.
- AI-generated content, edits, and publishing schedules.
- Social media account connection information and authorization tokens.
- Technical information, such as application logs and usage records.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Process uploaded content.
- Generate and improve social media content.
- Manage brand profiles and content libraries.
- Schedule and facilitate authorized social media publishing.
- Maintain application security and troubleshoot errors.

## 4. AI Services and Third-Party Platforms

ContentFlow AI may use third-party AI providers, cloud storage services, and social media platforms to provide its features.

When these integrations are enabled, relevant information may be transmitted to those providers to perform the requested operation. Their own privacy policies and terms also apply.

## 5. Social Media Connections

Social media integrations require user authorization. Users should connect only accounts they own or are authorized to manage.

Users may revoke application access through the relevant social media platform.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are intended to protect stored information, including access controls and secure handling of authentication credentials.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is intended to be retained only as long as necessary for the application's operation and legitimate project requirements.

For questions or requests concerning personal information, contact us using the email below.

## 8. Children's Privacy

ContentFlow AI is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect their personal information.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ContentFlow AI develops and its features or integrations change. Updates will be published on this page.
